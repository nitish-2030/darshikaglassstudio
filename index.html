About the Studio

Built on craftsmanship, run like a workshop.

Darshika Glass Studio is a local studio in Sidhi, built on 7+ years of hands-on glass and aluminium work before we set up here. We're a small, hands-on team — when you call, you speak directly to the people who will measure your space and install the work themselves.

We specialise in customised glass, aluminium and interior solutions for homes, offices, shops and commercial spaces, planning each job around how the space is actually used rather than fitting it to a standard template.
