Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

What information we collect

When you use our "Request a Free Quote" form, we ask for your name, phone number, service type, and an optional message describing your space.

How we use it

This form does not send your information to any server or database owned by us. When you click "Send Enquiry on WhatsApp," your browser opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled into a message. The message is sent only if you personally tap send inside WhatsApp. We receive it the same way as if you had messaged us directly — nothing is transmitted until that point.

Third-party services

Our website uses a small number of outside services to function:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — to receive your enquiry, subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — to load the fonts used on this site, which may log your IP address when the fonts are requested.
  • Google Maps — embedded on our Contact page to show our location, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Website hosting provider — this website is hosted on a third-party hosting platform (such as Cloudflare Pages, Netlify or Vercel), which may log basic technical data like IP address and browser type as part of standard web server operation, subject to that provider's own privacy policy.

We do not use these services to track you beyond what is required to load them.

Data we store

We do not maintain a database of form submissions. Once your WhatsApp message reaches us, it is stored only within WhatsApp on our own phone, like any other WhatsApp conversation you'd have with us.

Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts and the map embed may set minimal technical cookies required for them to load, controlled by Google, not by us.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete any WhatsApp conversation history with you by contacting us directly. You are never required to use the form to reach us — you're welcome to call or WhatsApp us directly using the details below.
